- **Web dashboard v1**: `apps/web` serves the Metro home view with the four tiles (Attention, Flow, Spend, Metrics), most valuable first, each with its source and as-of stamp and its own drill-down route (`/attention`, `/flow`, `/spend`, `/metrics`) with a way back; every Attention item links to where it is acted on; tiles can be shown, hidden and reordered by keyboard, kept in `localStorage` and applied before the first paint; data is read only on the server, from the newest `snapshot-<ISO>.json` in `WATCHMAN_SNAPSHOT_DIR` or the labelled synthetic fixture, cached and re-read at least hourly, and a build-output check proves the location never reaches the client bundle. `packages/core` gains `latestSnapshotFileName`, `parseSnapshotText` and `sourceGaps` (zheref/watchman#4).
